# 15 Blog Post Titles + Outlines (target 800–1200 words each)

Each outline is a skeleton for YOU to write in your own voice/experience — this keeps content 100% original and passes AdSense's "unique, valuable content" requirement. Do not copy-paste generic AI text; add your own anecdotes, specific prices (updated to current year), and photos you've taken or have rights to use.

### CATEGORY: Budget Destinations

**1. "10 Budget Destinations in India Under ₹5,000 for a 3-Day Trip"**
- Intro: why budget travel doesn't mean compromising experience
- List format: 10 places (mix of hills, beaches, heritage towns — e.g., McLeodganj, Gokarna, Hampi, Pushkar, Rishikesh)
- For each: approx cost breakdown (travel/stay/food), best season, 1 unique thing to do
- Closing: general money-saving tip + CTA to related itinerary post (internal link)

- Table: destination vs estimated 2-day budget

**3. "Goa on a Shoestring: How I Explored Goa in Under ₹3,000 (Non-Party Edition)"**

- Budget hostels, local buses vs rentals, street food vs restaurants

- Common tourist traps to avoid

**4. "5 Budget Beach Destinations in South India Beyond Goa"**

## Notes on originality
- Write from real trips, research from official tourism sites (incredibleindia.gov.in, state tourism boards), and verified traveler forums — never copy phrasing from existing blogs.
- Update prices to current year and mention "prices as of [Month, Year], may vary" — this itself is a freshness/trust signal to both readers and AdSense reviewers.
- Add at least 1 original photo per post if possible (phone photos are fine) — this is one of the strongest signals against "scraped/AI-spun content" flags.
