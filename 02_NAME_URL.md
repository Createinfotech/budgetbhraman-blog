# Blog Name & URL Ideas

Pick a name that's short, memorable, and hints at "budget/India" without boxing you in too tightly (in case you expand later).

## Name suggestions
1. **RupeeTrails** — rupeetrails.blogspot.com
2. **BudgetBhraman** (Bhraman = Hindi/Sanskrit for "travel/wandering") — budgetbhraman.blogspot.com
3. **DesiWanderer** — desiwanderer.blogspot.com
4. **PocketPacked** — pocketpacked.blogspot.com
5. **TrainTicketTravels** (nostalgic India rail-travel angle) — traintickettravels.blogspot.com
6. **ShoestringSafar** (Safar = journey) — shoestringsafar.blogspot.com
7. **KamKharchaYatra** (Kam Kharcha = low cost, Yatra = journey) — kamkharchayatra.blogspot.com
8. **BackpackBharat** — backpackbharat.blogspot.com

## Recommendation
Go with **"BudgetBhraman"** or **"RupeeTrails"** — both are unique (low chance of trademark/name clash), easy to spell, instantly signal "budget + India travel," and sound credible/professional rather than gimmicky.

## Tips
- Check name isn't taken on Blogger, Instagram, and as a .com (even if you don't buy the domain now, reserving matching social handles early helps brand consistency, which is a soft trust signal).
- Later, once approved and earning, consider migrating to a custom domain like `budgetbhraman.com` — Blogger supports free custom domain mapping, and it looks more professional (also required if you want higher CPM ad networks down the line).
