# On-Page SEO Checklist (use for every post)

## Title Tag
- [ ] Primary keyword near the beginning (e.g., "Budget Goa Trip" not "Trip to Goa on a Budget: A Guide")
- [ ] Under 60 characters so it doesn't truncate in search results
- [ ] Includes a number, year, or specific hook where natural ("7-Day Rajasthan Itinerary Under ₹15,000")

## Meta Description
- [ ] 140-160 characters
- [ ] Includes primary keyword + a clear benefit/CTA ("Real costs, day-wise plan, and money-saving tips")
- [ ] Written uniquely per post — never reused across posts

## URL Slug
- [ ] Short, keyword-focused, lowercase, hyphen-separated (e.g., `/budget-goa-trip-under-3000`)
- [ ] No stop words or dates baked in unless necessary

## Headings
- [ ] One H1 (post title, handled by Blogger automatically)
- [ ] H2s for each major section (matches outline structure)
- [ ] H3s for sub-points under long H2 sections (e.g., under a destination list)
- [ ] Keyword variations naturally in at least 2-3 headings (avoid stuffing the exact phrase repeatedly)

## Image Alt Text
- [ ] Every image has descriptive alt text (e.g., "sunrise view from Triund trek Himachal" not "IMG_2031")
- [ ] Compress images before upload (TinyPNG/Squoosh) for page speed
- [ ] File names renamed to be descriptive before upload (`triund-trek-sunrise.jpg`)

## Internal Linking
- [ ] Link to at least 2-3 related posts within the content (e.g., a destination post links to its itinerary post and a packing guide)
- [ ] Use descriptive anchor text, not "click here"
- [ ] Link to About/Contact naturally where relevant (builds site structure)

## Content Quality
- [ ] 800-1200 words, genuinely useful (not padded)
- [ ] Includes specific numbers: costs, distances, timings (specificity = trust + originality signal)
- [ ] First 100 words clearly state what the post covers (helps both readers and search snippets)
- [ ] Proofread for grammar/spelling before publishing

## Labels/Categories (Blogger-specific)
- [ ] Assign 1-2 relevant labels per post (Budget Destinations, Itineraries, Travel Tips, Packing Guides, Food & Culture)
- [ ] Keep label names consistent across all posts (don't create a new label per post)
