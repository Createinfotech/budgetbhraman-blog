# Content Publishing Schedule (Pre-AdSense Application)

## Goal
Look like an active, established blog with genuine topical depth before applying — not a site thrown together overnight.

## Recommended cadence
- **Weeks 1-2**: Publish 3 posts/week (get to 6 posts fast, covering different categories — not all "budget destinations")
- **Weeks 3-5**: Publish 2 posts/week (reach 15-16 posts total, matching the titles list)
- **Week 6 onward**: Settle into 1-2 posts/week — this is also the pace to maintain *after* approval, since consistency matters for long-term traffic/RPM growth

## Before applying, make sure you have:
- [ ] Minimum 15-20 published, high-quality posts (800+ words each)
- [ ] Posts spread across at least 3-4 categories (shows topical breadth, not a one-trick blog)
- [ ] Blog is **at least 4-6 weeks old** with a visible publishing history (avoid applying on day 1 even if you have 15 posts — age + consistency both matter)
- [ ] All required pages live: About, Contact, Privacy Policy
- [ ] Working navigation menu
- [ ] No broken images/links (do a manual click-through before applying)
- [ ] Mobile view checked and looks clean

## After hitting ~15-18 posts and 4+ weeks of consistent activity
You're in a good position to apply for AdSense. Keep publishing on schedule *during* the review period (can take 1-14+ days) — an inactive site during review can itself be a soft negative signal.
