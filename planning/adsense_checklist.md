# AdSense Approval Checklist

## Site fundamentals
- [ ] Blog age: ideally 4-6+ weeks old with visible post history (not a hard rule, but reduces rejection risk)
- [ ] 15-20+ original, substantial posts (800+ words), no thin/duplicate content
- [ ] Clear niche focus (budget travel in India) — avoid mixing in unrelated topics
- [ ] Custom, working navigation menu across all pages
- [ ] Mobile-responsive theme (test on an actual phone, not just browser resize)
- [ ] Fast loading — check with PageSpeed Insights, fix anything scoring very poor
- [ ] No broken links, missing images, or "lorem ipsum" placeholder text anywhere

## Required pages
- [ ] About Us page — live and linked in nav/footer
- [ ] Contact Us page — live and linked, with a real way to reach you
- [ ] Privacy Policy page — live and linked, mentions AdSense/cookies usage (see `pages/privacy-policy.md`)
- [ ] (Optional but helpful) Disclaimer page if you ever do affiliate links or sponsored content later

## Content policy compliance
- [ ] No copied/scraped content from other blogs (each post must be genuinely written by you)
- [ ] No restricted content: adult content, violence, hate speech, illegal activity, excessive profanity
- [ ] No content promoting dangerous/illegal travel activities (e.g., trespassing, drug tourism)
- [ ] No copyrighted images used without rights (use your own photos or properly licensed free stock — Pexels/Unsplash/Pixabay)
- [ ] No clickbait titles that misrepresent post content

## Technical/account setup
- [ ] Blogger blog is publicly visible (not set to private/invite-only)
- [ ] Site verified in Google Search Console (helps indexing before/during review)
- [ ] AdSense account uses accurate personal details matching your identity (needed for payment later)
- [ ] Domain/blogspot URL is the actual final one you intend to keep (avoid renaming/migrating right after applying)

## Things that commonly cause rejection (avoid these)
- [ ] Applying with fewer than ~10 posts or posts under 300-400 words
- [ ] AI-generated content that reads generic/repetitive (vary sentence structure, add real specifics, personal voice)
- [ ] Excessive ads, pop-ups, or auto-redirect scripts already on the site before approval (Blogger's default theme won't have these, but avoid adding any third-party scripts)
- [ ] Missing or incomplete Privacy Policy/Contact page
- [ ] Site under construction / navigation leading to empty or "coming soon" pages

## After applying
- Keep publishing on your normal schedule — don't pause during the review window
- If rejected, read the specific reason in the email (usually "low value content," "insufficient content," or "site under construction") and address that exact issue before reapplying (Google allows reapplication after fixes)
