# Budget Travel India — Blogger Project Plan

**Niche chosen:** Budget Travel in India (solo + couple + family friendly tips)
**Why this niche:** Massive, evergreen search volume in India ("cheap trip to X", "budget itinerary"), low content-production cost (most posts can be written from general knowledge + light research, no gear reviews needed), and AdSense reviewers like sites with clear topical focus and genuine local value — easier to avoid "thin/duplicate content" rejections than a generic "travel tips" blog.

## Folder guide
- `01_THEME.md` — Blogger theme recommendation + setup notes
- `02_NAME_URL.md` — Blog name & URL ideas
- `03_POST_TITLES_OUTLINES.md` — 15 post titles with outlines
- `pages/about.md`, `pages/contact.md`, `pages/privacy-policy.md` — ready-to-paste page content
- `seo/onpage_checklist.md` — per-post SEO checklist
- `planning/publishing_schedule.md` — posting cadence before applying
- `planning/adsense_checklist.md` — approval requirements checklist
